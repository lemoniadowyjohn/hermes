# AX17 Door Trim Bracket Quality Procedure

## Assembly requirement
Torque requirement: 8.5 ± 0.5 Nm.
Fastener must be tightened with a calibrated torque tool.

## Dimensional inspection
Inspection dimension: 24.00 ± 0.20 mm.
Measure at inspection characteristic D1 after assembly.

## Traceability
Record component ID, batch ID, inspector ID, date, and measured value in the inspection record.
